Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and data from unauthorized access, damage, or disruption. Core principles include confidentiality, integrity, and availability, often referred to as the CIA triad. Common threats include malware, phishing, ransomware, and denial-of-service attacks. Defensive measures range from firewalls and encryption to multi-factor authentication and regular security audits. As organizations increasingly rely on interconnected systems, cybersecurity has become a shared responsibility spanning technical controls, employee awareness, and organizational policy.
