Data privacy regulation governs how organizations collect, store, process, and share personal information about individuals. Laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation in Europe grant individuals rights over their data, including access, correction, and deletion, while imposing obligations on organizations to justify data collection and secure it appropriately. Non-compliance can result in significant financial penalties. Data privacy has become closely linked with cybersecurity practices, since protecting personal data from breaches is often a legal requirement as well as a security concern.
